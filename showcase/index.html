    🏛️ The 7 Immutable Laws of Touch Physics

    1. Law of Direct Manipulation: Apply transition: none and CSS variables during drag/pinch; only ease on release.

    2. Law of Dynamic Boundary Hand-off: Delegate scroll gestures based on scrollTop ≤ 0 at boundary points.

    3. Law of Temporal Promotion: Gate long-press/drag behind 350ms with visual compression (.press-holding).

    4. Law of Orthogonal Axis Lock: Lock strictly to dominant vector over first 8px to eliminate diagonal interference.

    5. Law of Viewport Adaptability: Bind window.visualViewport resize events to counteract mobile virtual keyboards.

    6. Law of Kinetic Momentum: Regress velocity over trailing 120ms to prevent abrupt gesture freezes.

    7. Law of Single Pointer Pipeline: Eliminate fragmented Touch/Mouse dual-code using unified W3C Pointer Events.
